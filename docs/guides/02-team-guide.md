# Team Guide — Quick start

**Who this is for:** A guide who works **under a tour operator** (not self-employed on Pagoda).  
**You do not sign up on the public form.** Your operator sends you an **invite link**.

---

## What you do on Pagoda

1. Complete your profile (photo, login) from the **invite email**.
2. Keep your profile up to date in **Settings**.
3. Work on tours your **operator assigns** to you.
4. Respond to **jobs** when invited or when jobs are open on the board.

**You cannot:** add tours to the library, manage other guides, or link yourself to tours — your operator does that.

---

## Step 1 — Open your invite

1. Check email from your operator → **Complete my profile**.
2. Link looks like: `https://[your-site]/auth/guide-invite?token=...`

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Invite email (blur personal data)  
> _Save as: `team-guide-01-email.png`_

---

## Step 2 — Create your login

On the invite page:

1. Check the summary your operator already filled in.
2. Enter **your name**, **email**, **password** (8+ characters).
3. Click the photo area → upload **profile photo** (required).
4. Answer **video call with travel advisor?** (Yes / No).
5. Optional: short bio or intro video.
6. Click **Create account & save**.
7. Verify email if asked, then log in at **Guide login**.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Invite page with photo upload and video-call question  
> _Save as: `team-guide-02-invite.png`_

---

## Step 3 — Review your profile

1. Log in → **Settings** → **Profile** → **Guide profile**.
2. Confirm languages, destinations, bio, etc.
3. Click **Save guide profile** after any change.
4. Tell your operator when you are ready — they usually **publish** your public profile.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Settings → Guide profile (team guide view)  
> _Save as: `team-guide-03-settings.png`_

---

## Step 4 — Your public profile (after operator publishes)

- Your link: `https://[your-site]/g/g-xxxxxxxx` (operator shares or copies from My Guides).
- Badge **Provisional** is normal until Pagoda certifies you — you **cannot** change the badge yourself.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Public profile `/g/...` with tier and certification badges  
> _Save as: `team-guide-04-public-profile.png`_

---

## Step 5 — Jobs and tours

- **Which tours you appear on:** decided by your operator in **Tour assignments** — not by you.
- **Jobs:** use the guide **Landing** / job board to view and bid when jobs are available.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Guide landing or job detail  
> _Save as: `team-guide-05-jobs.png`_

---

## Quick reference

| Task | Where |
|------|--------|
| Update photo / bio | Settings → Guide profile |
| Login | Guide login |
| Get on a tour for agents | Ask operator → Tour assignments |
| Certification badge | Set by Pagoda — not in your settings |

---

## Common issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Invite link “invalid” | Ask operator for a **new invite** from My Guides |
| Profile empty after invite | Operator should **Edit** and **Save** in My Guides (old data issue) |
| Only **Settings** in menu | Wait for Pagoda **account approval** |
